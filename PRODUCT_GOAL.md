# Creator OS Product Goal

## North star
Build the most capable creator operating system for faceless video, digital products, market intelligence, repurposing and autonomous channel operations, with measurable advantages over Digital Maker.AI while preserving owner control, source grounding and free-first execution.

## Competitive benchmark
Digital Maker.AI currently markets faceless video, AI images, ebooks/spreadsheets/planners, websites, Prompt Maker, Faceless Finder, Trend Finder, Store Finder, competitor/ad research, AI chat, API/MCP integrations, recurring characters, teams and up-to-30-minute Premium/Ultimate video workflows.

Creator OS must meet or exceed that surface while differentiating on: autonomous cross-tool workflows, evidence-grounded research, editing/recovery, repurposing, analytics feedback, approval-gated publishing, local/free routing, provider failover, open integration APIs and transparent cost policy.

## Version program
- V1/V2: complete foundation, production, editing, repurposing, publishing, analytics, guarded motion.
- V3: integration platform, Opportunity Lab, richer voices, thumbnails and research intelligence.
- V4: multi-channel operating system, team workspaces, reusable templates and campaigns.
- V5: autonomous growth loops, experiment allocation, content portfolio planning and monetization intelligence.
- V6: plugin/agent marketplace, public API/MCP ecosystem and enterprise-grade observability.

## Definition of better
A release is better only when measured across capability breadth, production quality, editing control, automation depth, research evidence, cost efficiency, reliability, integrations and owner safety. Claims require tests or observable product evidence.
